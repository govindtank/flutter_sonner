## 1.0.0

- Initial release of `flutter_sonner`.
- **Card Stacking Depth**: Natural card stacking with spring physics, scale reduction (0.95x, 0.90x), and depth offset.
- **Interactive In-Toast Actions**: Styled `SonnerAction` buttons with instant tap handling.
- **Promise / Future Toasts**: Seamless `Sonner.promise(...)` lifecycle transitioning from loading spinner to success or error state in-place.
- **Toast Types**: Full support for `success`, `error`, `info`, `warning`, `loading`, and `custom` builders.
- **Natural Swipe-to-Dismiss**: Velocity-based horizontal fling with smooth fade-out.
- **Multi-Position Positioning**: `bottomRight`, `bottomCenter`, `bottomLeft`, `topRight`, `topCenter`, `topLeft`.
- **Expand on Hover / Long Press**: Unfolds stacked cards for easy inspection.
- **Rich Theming**: Dark obsidian, clean light, glassmorphic, and custom color palettes.
